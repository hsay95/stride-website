FAQ

Before you download.

Is Stride free?

Yes. The free plan locks one app and includes walking to earn, the weekly bank and your progress. Stride Pro ($4.99/month, $19.99/year, or $39.99 once, US prices) locks unlimited apps and whole categories.

Does Stride see what I do in my apps?

No. Apple's Screen Time framework is one-way: Stride can lock and unlock the apps you choose, but it can't see what you do inside them.

Do I need an account?

No. No sign-up, email or login. Your steps and app choices stay on your iPhone.

What if I really need an app right now?

Spend minutes from your bank, or take a short walk to earn more. You can also remove a lock at any time. Stride is a commitment device, not a cage.

Which phones does it work on?

iPhone with iOS 17.4 or later. Steps from an Apple Watch count too, through Apple Health. Stride isn't available on Android.
