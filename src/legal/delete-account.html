This page is for Stride: App Lock & Screen Time on Google Play and the Stride app on the App Store.

Stride works without an account. The only account is the optional Friends profile, created when you sign in with Apple (iPhone) or Google (Android). You can delete it anytime.

Delete it in the app

  • Open Stride, go to Friends › Edit profile › Delete my Friends account,
  • or Settings › Friends › Delete Friends account.

This deletes your Friends data from our server right away.

Delete it without the app

Email advantageyash@gmail.com from any address with the subject "Delete my Stride account". Tell us your Friends name and, if you can, your friend code, and say whether you signed in with Apple or Google. We'll confirm it's you, delete the account within 7 days, and reply when it's done.

Delete some data but keep your account

  • Usage analytics: in Stride, open Settings › Privacy and turn off Share anonymous usage data. Stride stops sending usage events and resets its random ID. To delete events already sent, email us (address above) with the subject "Delete my Stride usage data", and we'll delete them within 7 days.
  • Friends: remove a friend or block someone from their Friends card; they lose access to your steps and streak right away.
  • Health and Screen Time data: nothing to delete on our side. It stays on your phone. Revoke access in Health Connect or Apple Health, or uninstall Stride to remove it.

What gets deleted

Your Friends name and emoji, friend code, friends and requests, weekly step totals, usual weekly total, streak and goal days, weekly results, cheers, notification settings and device tokens, and the link to your Apple or Google account.

What's kept

Reports you made about other people are kept without your account, so we can still act on them. Anonymous usage analytics aren't linked to your account; see the privacy policy for how to clear them. Everything else Stride stores is on your phone and is removed when you uninstall the app.
