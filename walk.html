How it works

Set up in about two minutes

  1. Pick what to lock

    Allow Apple Health and Screen Time, then choose your app. It locks right away.

  2. Go for a walk

    Stride counts the steps your iPhone or Apple Watch records. Every 500 earns 15 minutes.

  3. Unlock, then relock

    Start a 15 minute pass when you want a break. When it's used, your apps lock again.

Questions people ask

How does Stride work?

Pick the apps you want to use less and Stride locks them with Apple's Screen Time. Every 500 steps you walk, counted by Apple Health, adds 15 minutes to your bank. Start a pass when you want a break. When the minutes are used, the apps lock again.

Is it free?

Yes, Stride is free to download and the free plan locks one app, including walking to earn and your weekly bank. Stride Pro locks unlimited apps and whole categories such as Social and Games. It costs $4.99 a month, $19.99 a year or $39.99 once (US prices). Apple handles every purchase.

Does Stride see what I do in my apps?

No. Apple's Screen Time system lets Stride lock and unlock the apps you choose, but Stride can't see which apps you use or what you do inside them.

Do I need an account?

No. Stride works with no sign up, email or login. Only the optional Friends leaderboard uses Sign in with Apple, and it shares just your weekly step total and streak with friends you approve.

Which steps count?

Steps your iPhone or Apple Watch records in Apple Health. Steps typed in by hand in the Health app don't count toward earned minutes.

What happens when my minutes run out?

Your locked apps lock again on their own. Stride sends a heads up when one minute is left and another when your minutes are used. Minutes only count down while you're using locked apps.

Do unused minutes expire?

Earned minutes stay in your bank for the whole week and reset every Monday, so a long Monday walk can cover a few breaks later in the week.

Which devices does Stride work on?

iPhone with iOS 17.4 or later. Stride isn't available for iPad or Android yet.
