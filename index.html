FAQ

Questions, answered.

How does Stride work?

Pick the apps you want to use less and Stride locks them with Apple's Screen Time. Every 500 steps you walk, counted by Apple Health, adds 15 minutes to your bank. Start a pass when you want a break; when the minutes are used, the apps lock again.

Does Stride see what I do in my apps?

No. Apple's Screen Time framework is one-way: Stride can lock and unlock the apps you choose, but it can't see which apps you use or what you do inside them.

Do I need an account?

No. Stride works with no sign-up, email or login, and your steps, app choices and minutes stay on your iPhone. Only the optional Friends leaderboard uses Sign in with Apple, and it shares just your weekly step total and streak with friends you approve.

Which steps count?

Steps your iPhone or Apple Watch records in Apple Health. Steps typed in by hand in the Health app don't count toward earned minutes.

What happens when my minutes run out?

Your locked apps lock again on their own. Stride sends a heads-up when one minute is left and another when your minutes are used. Minutes only count down while you're using locked apps.

Do unused minutes expire?

Earned minutes stay in your bank for the whole week and reset every Monday, so a long Monday walk can cover a few breaks later in the week.

What's the difference between Free and Pro?

Free locks one app and includes everything else: walking to earn, the weekly bank and your progress. Stride Pro ($4.99/month, $19.99/year, or $39.99 once) locks unlimited apps and whole categories such as Social, Games or Entertainment.

Can Stride block websites?

No. Stride locks apps and app categories. To block a website in Safari, use Settings › Screen Time › Content & Privacy Restrictions.

Which devices does Stride work on?

iPhone with iOS 17.4 or later. Stride isn't available for iPad or Android.

How do I cancel or restore Stride Pro?

Purchases are handled by Apple. To cancel, open the App Store, tap your profile picture, then Subscriptions. To restore a purchase, open Stride's Settings tab and tap Restore Purchases.
