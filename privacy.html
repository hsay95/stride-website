Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Stride ("we", "us") provides the Stride iOS app ("the app"). This policy explains what data the app uses and how. Stride is designed to keep your data on your device.

Summary

  • We do not run our own servers for your data and we do not build personal profiles.
  • Your health and Screen Time data stay on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Two service providers process limited data for us: RevenueCat (with Apple) to manage purchases, and PostHog for anonymous usage analytics.
  • Usage analytics are anonymous, contain no health or Screen Time data, and can be turned off in the app.
  • We do not sell your data or use it for advertising or tracking.

1. Health data (Apple Health / HealthKit)

With your permission, Stride reads your step count from Apple Health to calculate how many minutes of app time you've earned. Access is read-only. Stride never writes data to Apple Health. Manually entered Health steps are excluded from earned minutes. Your Health data is processed on your device and is never sent to us, never included in analytics, never used for advertising or marketing, and never shared or sold.

2. Screen Time data (Family Controls / Device Activity)

Stride uses Apple's Screen Time framework only to block the apps you choose and to re-lock them when your earned time runs out. Apple's framework is privacy-protected and one-directional: Stride cannot see which apps you use, your browsing, or your Screen Time numbers. It can only display Apple-rendered Screen Time summaries to you; even the app itself cannot read those numbers. Your app selections are stored only on your device and are never included in analytics.

3. Information stored on your device

Your chosen display name, app selections, earned/used minutes, step history, and preferences are stored locally on your device and in a private app group shared only with Stride's own extensions. This data is not transmitted to us.

4. Subscriptions and purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription entitlements, such as whether you have Stride Pro. RevenueCat may process purchase information and a randomized app/device identifier to deliver this service. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy. We do not receive your name, email, or payment details from Apple.

5. Anonymous usage analytics

To understand which parts of Stride work and which confuse people, the app sends anonymous usage events to PostHog, our analytics provider. For example: which setup screen you reached, whether a permission was allowed, whether you chose an app to lock, started an app-time pass, or opened the upgrade screen, and whether a purchase completed.

Each event can include:

  • the event name and simple details such as the screen, plan or result;
  • how many apps or categories you lock (a count, never which ones);
  • a random ID created for this install of the app, so events from one install can be grouped;
  • device model, iOS version, app version and build (including whether it is a TestFlight build), language, time zone, screen size, and whether you are on Wi-Fi or cellular.

Analytics never include:

  • your name, email, contacts or Apple ID;
  • step counts, earned or used minutes, streaks or any other data from Apple Health;
  • Screen Time data, or the apps, categories or websites you choose;
  • your location, your IP address (PostHog discards it), or your device's advertising identifier;
  • screen recordings or what you type.

The random ID is not linked to you, to your purchases or to any account, and we do not use analytics to track you across other companies' apps or websites. PostHog stores this data in the United States on our behalf. We keep it only as long as it helps us improve Stride. See PostHog's privacy policy.

Turning it off: open Stride, then Settings › Privacy, and switch off "Share anonymous usage data". The app then stops sending analytics and forgets its random ID.

6. Notifications

Stride sends local notifications, such as session reminders, from your device. These are not sent through our servers.

7. Data we do not collect

We do not collect accounts, contacts, location, advertising identifiers, health data or Screen Time data. We have no login. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell or share it with data brokers.

8. Children

Stride is not directed to children under 13. If Stride is used by a minor under a Family Sharing organizer, the organizer is responsible for that use.

9. Your choices

  • Turn off anonymous usage analytics anytime in Stride's Settings › Privacy.
  • Revoke Apple Health or Screen Time access anytime in iOS Settings.
  • Delete all locally stored data by deleting the app.
  • Because analytics are anonymous, we can't find your events from your name or email. To ask us to delete them, turn analytics off (which resets the random ID) or email us and we'll help.

10. Changes

We may update this policy; we'll change the "Last updated" date above.
