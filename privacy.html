Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Stride ("we", "us") provides the Stride iOS app ("the app"). This policy explains what data the app uses and how. Stride is designed to keep your data on your device.

Summary

  • Stride works without an account. If you choose to join Friends, we keep a small Friends profile on our server so the friends you approve can see your weekly step total and your daily-goal streak.
  • Apart from those optional Friends figures, your health and Screen Time data stay on your iPhone.
  • Service providers that process limited data for us: RevenueCat (with Apple) to manage purchases, PostHog for anonymous usage analytics, and Supabase to host Friends.
  • Usage analytics are anonymous, contain no health or Screen Time data, and can be turned off in the app.
  • We do not sell your data or use it for advertising or tracking.

1. Health data (Apple Health / HealthKit)

With your permission, Stride reads your step count from Apple Health to calculate how many minutes of app time you've earned. Access is read-only. Stride never writes data to Apple Health. Manually entered Health steps are excluded from earned minutes. Your Health data is processed on your device. If you join Friends, only your weekly step totals, your usual weekly total, your streak and which days you reached your daily goal are uploaded, as described in "Friends" below. Health data is never included in analytics, never used for advertising or marketing, and never sold.

2. Screen Time data (Family Controls / Device Activity)

Stride uses Apple's Screen Time framework only to block the apps you choose and to re-lock them when your earned time runs out. Apple's framework is privacy-protected and one-directional: Stride cannot see which apps you use, your browsing, or your Screen Time numbers. It can only display Apple-rendered Screen Time summaries to you; even the app itself cannot read those numbers. Your app selections are stored only on your device and are never included in analytics or Friends.

3. Friends (optional)

Friends is an optional weekly step leaderboard with people you approve. Stride works fully without it.

  • Age. Friends is for people 13 and older. Before you sign in, the app asks for your birth month and year. It keeps only whether you can join, on your iPhone, and never sends your birth date to us.
  • Signing in. Joining Friends uses Sign in with Apple. Apple gives us an account ID and, if you allow it, your name and email address (you can hide your email with Apple's private relay address). We keep a Sign in with Apple token only so we can revoke Stride's access when you delete your Friends account.
  • What we store: your Friends name and emoji, your friend code, your friends and pending friend requests, your total steps for each Monday–Sunday week, your usual weekly total (an average of your previous weeks, used for "Most improved"), your streak length and which days you reached the 5,000-step daily goal (yes or no per day, not the step count), your weekly results (rank and crowns), the preset cheers you send and receive, your notification settings and time zone, and a notification token for each device you use. If you block someone, we store that block; if you report someone, we store the report (the reason, any note you add, and the name they used).
  • Who sees it: only people you have accepted as friends see your weekly total, streak, whether you reached today's goal, and the cheers you send them. Their other friends don't see you. Someone you give your friend code to sees your name and emoji so they can send you a request. There are no public profiles and Stride never reads your contacts.
  • The "you out-walked X% of Stride walkers" line is calculated from everyone's weekly totals and never shows anyone's name.
  • Your daily step counts and the rest of your Health data never leave your iPhone.
  • Safety: names that are offensive or contain links or contact details are rejected. You can block or report anyone from their Friends card or their request. Blocking removes you from each other's boards and stops new requests, and they aren't told. We review reports within 24 hours and may rename or remove accounts that break these rules.
  • Where it's kept: Friends data is hosted by Supabase on servers in India (Mumbai). Friends notifications are delivered by Apple Push Notification service.
  • Deleting it: anytime, in Friends › Edit profile › Delete my Friends account, or in Settings › Friends › Delete Friends account. This deletes everything above from our server right away and revokes Stride's Sign in with Apple access. Reports you made about others are kept without your account so we can still act on them.

4. Information stored on your device

Your chosen display name, app selections, earned/used minutes, step history, and preferences are stored locally on your device and in a private app group shared only with Stride's own extensions. Apart from the optional Friends data above, this data is not transmitted to us.

5. Subscriptions and purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription entitlements, such as whether you have Stride Pro. RevenueCat may process purchase information and a randomized app/device identifier to deliver this service. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy. We do not receive your name, email, or payment details from Apple for purchases, and purchases are not linked to your Friends account.

6. Anonymous usage analytics

To understand which parts of Stride work and which confuse people, the app sends anonymous usage events to PostHog, our analytics provider. For example: which setup screen you reached, whether a permission was allowed, whether you chose an app to lock, started an app-time pass, opened the upgrade screen or used a Friends feature, and whether a purchase completed.

Each event can include:

  • the event name and simple details such as the screen, plan or result;
  • how many apps or categories you lock (a count, never which ones);
  • a random ID created for this install of the app, so events from one install can be grouped;
  • device model, iOS version, app version and build (including whether it is a TestFlight build), language, time zone, screen size, and whether you are on Wi-Fi or cellular.

Analytics never include:

  • your name, email, contacts, Apple ID or Friends account;
  • step counts, earned or used minutes, streaks or any other data from Apple Health;
  • Screen Time data, or the apps, categories or websites you choose;
  • your location, your IP address (PostHog discards it), or your device's advertising identifier;
  • screen recordings or what you type.

The random ID is not linked to you, to your purchases or to your Friends account, and we do not use analytics to track you across other companies' apps or websites. PostHog stores this data in the United States on our behalf. We keep it only as long as it helps us improve Stride. See PostHog's privacy policy.

Turning it off: open Stride, then Settings › Privacy, and switch off "Share anonymous usage data". The app then stops sending analytics and forgets its random ID.

7. Notifications

Stride sends local notifications, such as session reminders, from your device. These are not sent through our servers.

If you join Friends, we send push notifications through Apple when someone asks to be your friend or accepts your request, when a friend passes you or cheers you on, near the end of the week, with your weekly result, and when a streak is about to end. You can turn each kind off in Friends › Edit profile, and we don't send them between 10 pm and 8 am your time.

8. Data we do not collect

We do not collect contacts, location, advertising identifiers or Screen Time data, and outside the optional Friends feature we do not collect accounts or health data. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell or share it with data brokers.

9. Children

Stride is not directed to children under 13. Outside Friends there are no accounts, and the first name you can type during setup stays on your iPhone. Friends asks for a birth month and year before sign-in and isn't available to anyone under 13. If we learn that a child under 13 has a Friends account, we delete it and its data. A parent or guardian who believes this has happened can email us at the address below. If Stride is used by a minor under a Family Sharing organizer, the organizer is responsible for that use.

10. Your choices

  • Use Stride without Friends; joining is optional.
  • Delete your Friends data anytime in the app, as described in "Friends".
  • Block or report anyone on Friends from their card or request.
  • Turn off anonymous usage analytics anytime in Stride's Settings › Privacy.
  • Revoke Apple Health or Screen Time access anytime in iOS Settings.
  • Delete all locally stored data by deleting the app.
  • Because analytics are anonymous, we can't find your events from your name or email. To ask us to delete them, turn analytics off (which resets the random ID) or email us and we'll help.

11. Changes

We may update this policy; we'll change the "Last updated" date above.
