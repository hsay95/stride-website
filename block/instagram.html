Setup

How to block Instagram with Stride

  1. Get Stride from the App StoreIt's free to download, and there's no account to create.
  2. Allow Apple Health and Screen TimeHealth shares your step count, read-only. Screen Time lets Stride lock apps. Both stay on your iPhone.
  3. Choose Instagram to lockIn the Apps tab, pick Instagram in Apple's app picker. It locks right away. With Pro, add more apps or a whole category.
  4. Walk, then unlockEvery 500 steps adds 15 minutes to your bank. Tap Start 15 min app pass on the Today tab, and Instagram relocks when the time is used.

FAQ

Blocking Instagram: common questions

Can I block Instagram for free?

Yes. Stride's free plan locks one app, and Instagram can be that app. Stride Pro ($4.99/month, $19.99/year or $39.99 once) adds unlimited apps and whole categories.

Can Stride see what I do on Instagram?

No. Apple's Screen Time framework gives Stride an anonymous token for Instagram. Stride can lock and unlock the app, but it can't see your feed, your messages or anything else you do inside it.

Does Stride block Instagram in Safari too?

No. Stride locks the Instagram app. To block instagram.com in Safari as well, add it as a restricted website in Settings › Screen Time › Content & Privacy Restrictions.

What happens when my Instagram time runs out?

Instagram locks again on its own. Stride sends a heads-up when one minute is left and another when your minutes are used up.

What if I really need Instagram right now?

If you have minutes banked, start a 15-minute pass from the Today tab. If not, a short walk earns more. You can also remove the lock in the Apps tab at any time. Stride is a commitment device, not a cage.

Does locking Instagram also lock Threads or Facebook?

Only if you pick them too. Threads and Facebook are separate apps in Apple's app picker. The free plan locks one app; Stride Pro locks as many as you like, or a whole category such as Social.
